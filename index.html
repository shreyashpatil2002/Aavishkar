Aavishkar is an annual event organized by our college, featuring a variety of competitions and activities in technical, non-technical, and extracurricular fields, aiming to foster innovation, creativity, and skill development among students.

Aavishkar features a wide range of events, including technical competitions (coding, robotics), non-technical events (quizzes, presentations), hackathons, workshops, and extracurricular activities (interview experiences, networking sessions).

Some events may require prior knowledge or experience, especially technical competitions. However, many events are open to all skill levels. Please check the event descriptions for specific requirements.
